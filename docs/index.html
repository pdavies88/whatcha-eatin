Whatcha Eatin'

Snap your plate. See the macros.

Whatcha Eatin' is an iPhone and Android app that estimates the calories, protein, carbs and fat in a photo of your meal. It keeps the last seven days of meals on your phone and clears older ones automatically.

  • No accounts or subscriptions. You use your own AI key. A Google Gemini key is free.
  • Your choice of AI. Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI or OpenRouter.
  • Private by design. No servers, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • A rolling week. A 7-day calendar of meals with daily totals.
